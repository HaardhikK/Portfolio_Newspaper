Alvalta is a small art experiment in artificial life. I wanted to work near that idea without building a full biological simulation or pretending three equations are alive. Motion, repetition, and character seemed like a more honest place to begin.

The piece is made of three original point-creatures, each generated by its own equation and moving on its own internal rhythm. They share one dark field, where they cross, drift beyond the boundary, and pass through one another. None of them reacts intelligently. What interests me is how little structure it takes before motion starts to read as presence.

Clicking a creature reveals the equation it runs. The image is the work, but the mechanism is there for anyone who wants to look.
