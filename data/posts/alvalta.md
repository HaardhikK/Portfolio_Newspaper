Alvalta is a small art experiment in artificial life. 

The piece is made of three original point-creatures, each generated by its own equation and moving on its own internal rhythm. They share one dark field, where they cross, drift beyond the boundary and pass through one another. What interests me is how little structure it takes before motion starts to read as presence.

Clicking a creature reveals the equation it runs. Base inspiration from @yuruyurau. Designs used here are original.
